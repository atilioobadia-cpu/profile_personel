Mwanza · TZ

SECTOR|Tilapia fish farming, multi-branch

STACK|ERPNext · Frappe · Simulation engine

Problem

Costs were tracked manually across branches. Nobody could say what each farm site was really spending or producing without days of reconciliation.

Section 05 / Profile Record

About

REF | BIO-2026

> I started out in general IT support — fixing machines, setting up networks, and configuring biometric devices for offices that needed attendance and access control to just work. That hardware-and-networks foundation still shapes how I build today: systems have to run in the real world, on real connections, for real people.

From there I moved into full web and systems development — building websites, inventory platforms and portals for clients who had outgrown paper and spreadsheets. Somewhere along the way, nearly every custom system started asking the same questions an accounting system is built to answer, and that pulled me deep into ERPNext.

Today I consult on ERPNext and the Frappe framework under my brand, ERPcodec — deploying, customising and integrating it for businesses in Mwanza and across East Africa. My favourite projects are the ones where messy manual processes become clean, auditable records: a receipt printed for every entry, a ledger anyone can trust.
