Section 02 / The Ledger

Case Studies

REF | REC-INDEX-2026

REC-01

TBBCL — The Big Best Company Ltd

Tanzania · EAC

SECTOR|Tilapia fish farming, multi-branch

STACK|ERPNext · Frappe · Simulation engine

Problem

Costs were tracked manually across branches. Nobody could say what each farm site was really spending or producing without days of reconciliation.

Built

A fish production simulation system paired with a full ERPNext deployment — tailored chart of accounts and multi-branch cost accounting built around aquaculture reality.

Result

Branch-level cost visibility leadership can trust. Reconciliation that took days now takes minutes — decisions run on live records, not guesswork.

REC-02

AATL — Alpha Associates Tanzania Ltd

Tanzania

SECTOR|Accounting & tax advisory

STACK|Web · Custom system · ERPNext

Problem

No digital foundation at all — operations ran on paper and spreadsheets, with zero online presence in an industry that sells trust.

Built

A company website, a custom internal working system, and an ERPNext accounting deployment complete with a performance evaluation setup.

Result

Digital operations organized end-to-end, plus a professional online presence their clients can actually find. Paper files fully retired; monthly reporting prep cut roughly in half.

REC-03

PMM Tanzania — ICD System

Contributor

ROLE|Contributing developer

STACK|REST APIs · Integrations

Problem

The ICD platform needed capability beyond its current feature set — and needed to exchange data with external services.

Built

Extended the system as a contributing developer — REST API endpoints and integration work connecting it to surrounding tools.

Result

New functionality shipped upstream; the system now talks to the platforms around it instead of standing alone.

REC-04

Mtokoma Inventory System

Standalone Build

SECTOR|Trade & retail inventory

STACK|Custom web system

Problem

Stock tracked by hand and paper. Counts drifted, losses stayed invisible until they were expensive, and audits meant flipping notebooks.

Built

A standalone inventory system covering items, movements and stock levels — every transaction written down as a proper record.

Result

Manual tracking fully replaced. Every item movement leaves a traceable entry behind it — stock counts that dragged past close are now same-day.

Section 07 / Profile Record

About

REF | BIO-2026

> I started out in general IT support — fixing machines, setting up networks, and configuring biometric devices for offices that needed attendance and access control to just work. That hardware-and-networks foundation still shapes how I build today: systems have to run in the real world, on real connections, for real people.

From there I moved into full web and systems development — building websites, inventory platforms and portals for clients who had outgrown paper and spreadsheets. Somewhere along the way, nearly every custom system started asking the same questions an accounting system is built to answer, and that pulled me deep into ERPNext.

Today I work as an Open Source System Developer under my brand, ERPcodec — deploying, customising and integrating open platforms like ERPNext and Frappe for businesses across Tanzania and the wider East Africa Community. My favourite projects are the ones where messy manual processes become clean, auditable records: a receipt printed for every entry, a ledger anyone can trust.
